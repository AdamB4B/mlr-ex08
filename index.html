    Privacy

    Everything runs on your device. The camera frames and your photos are read into a canvas, turned into a 1 × 3 × 96 × 96 tensor and classified by the ONNX model in your browser using WebAssembly. No image is ever uploaded.

    The page loads only: the two JavaScript libraries listed in the HTML head (jsDelivr CDN) and the model files from this same site. There is no analytics, no tracking and no server-side inference.
